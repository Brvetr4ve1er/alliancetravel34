# Visa Research — 10 Countries x Algerian Applicants
Researched: 2026-06-03 · For Alliance Travel rendez-vous-visa page

> **Internal note for the page builder:** never publish hard fee/timeline promises. All timelines below are *embassy/consulate-published ranges*. Agency value-add is "we book the slot, we audit the dossier, we drop it off where allowed" — not "we guarantee the visa."

## Top-level patterns

Algerian applicants share a small set of outsourced intake networks. For Schengen, **France migrated entirely to Capago in April 2025** (Algiers, Oran, Annaba, Constantine), while **Germany still routes through VFS Global Algiers** and **Spain runs through BLS International Algiers + Oran**. Türkiye uses the Mosaic Visa centre in Algiers as its official intake partner. China runs through the CVASC Chinese Visa Application Service Center co-located with the Chinese Embassy in Ben Aknoun. Russia is **embassy-only** in El-Biar (no outsourced VAC, no e-visa). Egypt is also embassy-only, with a tour-operator-led visa-on-arrival exception. Saudi Arabia tourism is **not e-visa eligible for Algerian ordinary passports** — Algerians must apply at the embassy unless they hold a qualifying Schengen/UK/US visa or GCC residency. The United States is interview-based at the US Embassy Algiers, with a new bond pilot affecting Algerian B1/B2 applicants since January 2026. Canada uses the VFS Global VAC in Ben Aknoun, Algiers — biometrics are collected on-site; consular processing itself is handled by IRCC offices outside Algeria (Rabat / Paris network, [UNVERIFIED specific 2026 routing]). Algerians benefit from one growing trend: most Schengen + UK + US + Canadian visa biometric prints stay valid 59 months across multiple applications.

### 1. France · Schengen short-stay (C-type)

**Where:** **Capago International** is the sole official intake partner since 07 April 2025, replacing VFS Global and TLScontact. Four centres: Algiers (Pins Maritimes, Mohammadia), Oran (Bir El Djir), Annaba (Section 21), Constantine (Zone Boussouf). Application starts on the France-Visas portal (france-visas.gouv.fr), then booking is on fr-dz.capago.eu [1][2][3].

**Visa types:** Short-stay Schengen (C-type tourism/family/business), long-stay national (D-type for students/work/family reunification).

**Document checklist (headline):**
- Passport valid 3+ months beyond planned return, 2 blank pages [1]
- France-Visas online application + bar-coded confirmation page [1]
- 2x biometric photos (35x45mm, white background, recent) [1]
- Travel medical insurance, min EUR 30,000 coverage, Schengen-wide [1]
- Confirmed round-trip flight reservation (booking, not paid ticket) [1]
- Hotel booking / attestation d'hébergement legalized by mairie [1]
- 3 months bank statements + proof of employment (CNAS or work attestation, payslips) [1]
- Family civil-status documents (acte de naissance, livret de famille) [1]

**Processing time range:** Schengen Visa Code mandates a decision within 15 calendar days of submission; in practice the consulate may extend to 45 days, occasionally 60, for additional review. Booking the slot itself can take 1+ month during peak (June-November) [3].

**Biometric:** Yes, mandatory on-site at Capago. As of 20 Feb 2026, biometrics are required worldwide for all French visas unless prints are <59 months old on file [4].

**e-visa:** No.

**2026 caveats:** Capago charges EUR 29 in mandatory service fees on top of consular fees. Oran applicants must now book online (mandatory online slots rolled out late 2025, expected to extend to all centres in 2026) [3][5].

**Sources:** [1] france-visas.gouv.fr/en/algerie · [2] insurte.com (Capago replacement notice) · [3] fr-dz.capago.eu · [4] visahq.com (France mandatory biometrics worldwide, Feb 2026) · [5] visahq.com (Capago Oran online appointments, Nov 2025)

### 2. Türkiye · Sticker visa (e-visa conditional)

**Where:** Mosaic Visa is the authorised Türkiye Visa Application Centre in Algiers (Haouch Semmar, Birkhadem). Decisions remain with the Consulate General of Türkiye; Mosaic is intake-only [6].

**Visa types:** Sticker tourist/business visa (air or land). Conditional 30-day e-Visa available via evisa.gov.tr only for Algerians ages 15-18 or 35-65 who also hold a valid Schengen/UK/US/Ireland visa or residence permit [7].

**Document checklist (headline):**
- Original passport, 6+ months validity from intended arrival, 2 blank pages [6]
- 2 biometric photos, 5x6cm, white background, taken within last 6 months [6]
- Original + copy of Algerian National ID card [6]
- Flight reservation (booking only) in applicant's full legal name [6]
- Hotel booking / tenancy contract / notarized invitation letter [6]
- 6 months bank statements (closing date <10 days old), sufficient closing balance [6]
- Employment proof: employer letter, CNAS declaration, 3 months payslips (or alt by status) [6]
- For minors: parental authorization, stamped by municipality [6]

**Processing time range:** Mosaic does not publish a fixed range. Industry practice for Türkiye sticker visas runs 5-15 business days subject to consulate workload [UNVERIFIED — Mosaic site does not commit; recommend caveat language only].

**Biometric:** Yes — sticker visa applicants must appear in person for fingerprint enrolment [6].

**e-visa:** Conditional only (see eligibility above) [7].

**2026 caveats:** Algerian ordinary-passport holders under 15 or over 65 may enter visa-free for up to 90 days within any 180-day period for tourism [7].

**Sources:** [6] mosaicvisa.com/algiers · [7] mfa.gov.tr/visa-information-for-foreigners.en.mfa

### 3. Germany · Schengen short-stay (C-type)

**Where:** VFS Global Algiers handles intake for the German Embassy. Application starts with the VIDEX form (online), printed and submitted at VFS [8][9].

**Visa types:** Schengen short-stay (tourism, business, visit), national long-stay (D).

**Document checklist (headline):**
- Signed VIDEX application form, single copy [8]
- Passport valid 3+ months beyond visa expiry, copy of all stamped pages [8]
- Copy of previous passport if current is <1 year old [8]
- Biometric photo (recent, bright background, uncovered face) [8]
- Schengen-wide travel insurance, min EUR 30,000, covering medical + repatriation [8]
- 3 months Algerian bank statements + euro-denominated proof of means [8]
- Hotel bookings + round-trip flight reservation [8]
- Employment letter, CNAS, 3 months payslips OR commercial register if self-employed [8]

**Processing time range:** Type-C short-stay: 15 calendar days from submission per Schengen Visa Code (consulate may extend). Submit earliest 6 months before, latest 15 days before travel [8].

**Biometric:** Yes — VFS collects fingerprints and digital photo on-site [9].

**e-visa:** No.

**Fee:** EUR 80 equivalent, paid in DZD cash at VFS [8].

**2026 caveats:** None specific to Algeria identified.

**Sources:** [8] visa.vfsglobal.com/one-pager/germany/algeria/english · [9] visa.vfsglobal.com/dza/en/deu

### 4. Spain · Schengen short-stay (C-type)

**Where:** BLS International on behalf of the Consulate of Spain. Two centres: Algiers + Oran. All appointments booked exclusively via algeria.blsspainvisa.com [10][11].

**Visa types:** Schengen tourism, visit (visa de visita), business, transit.

**Document checklist (headline):**
- National application form, signed [10]
- Passport (full validity rules per Schengen) — if passport issued in 2025/2026, applicants must also submit a copy of all pages of the old passport [11]
- Recent biometric photo per ICAO spec [10]
- Recent civil-status documents (family records) [10]
- 3 months bank statements, in both DZD and EUR equivalent [10]
- Mandatory Schengen travel insurance certificate [10]
- Confirmed round-trip flight tickets [10]
- All documents in French or Spanish; Arabic originals need translation [10]

**Processing time range:** Minimum 15 calendar days, "may delay depending on case to case" per BLS; satellite-to-consulate transit adds 48-72h; busy-period range 30-45 days [10][12].

**Biometric:** Yes — fingerprints + photo on-site if not on file. Prints stay valid 59 months [12].

**e-visa:** No.

**2026 caveats:** New ALG1-ALG4 appointment categories effective 01 Jan 2026, based on Algerian applicant's prior Schengen-Spain visa history. Applicants whose last Schengen visa was issued by another member state must book in the ALG1 (first-time) category. Algiers centre accepts applicants whose travel is within 30 days; Oran centre accepts up to 90 days. Appointment release time moved to 18h00 effective 09 April 2026 [11].

**Sources:** [10] algeria.blsspainvisa.com/algiers/tourism.php · [11] algeria.blsspainvisa.com/algiers (2026 ALG categories notice) · [12] hellosafe.com/schengen-visa/bls-international (general BLS process)

### 5. China (PRC) · Tourist L visa

**Where:** **Chinese Visa Application Service Center (CVASC)** — Cité Houidef Bouteldja N°1, Ben Aknoun, Alger. Hours: Sunday and Thursday 09:00-15:00 for submission AND collection. Online pre-form at COVA (cova.mfa.gov.cn) is mandatory before in-person appointment [13][14].

**Visa types:** L (tourism), M (business/commerce), F (short-term exchange), Z/X (work/study — out of scope).

**Document checklist (headline) for L-visa:**
- Original passport, 6+ months remaining validity, blank pages [15]
- Passport bio-page photocopy [15]
- COVA-generated visa application form (printed) [15]
- Recent passport-size colour photo, white background [15]
- Round-trip flight booking + hotel reservation for full stay [15]
- OR invitation letter from individual/entity in China with inviter contact + stamp/signature [15]
- Employer certification letter with income + bank statement [15]
- Proof of legal residence in Algeria if applicant is non-Algerian national [15]

**Processing time range:** Standard 4 working days from CVASC submission; rush/express services available at extra cost [15]. [UNVERIFIED — Algiers CVASC may have local variances; recommend caveat in copy.]

**Biometric:** [UNVERIFIED — Algiers CVASC fingerprint requirement not confirmed in fetched sources. Note that worldwide PRC visa centres have rolled out fingerprint collection 2019+, but the Algiers centre's current 2026 policy was not retrievable from official channels during research. Suggest CTA copy: "biometric possible — check at appointment."]

**e-visa:** No e-visa for Algerians [16][17]. Algeria is explicitly excluded from China's unilateral 30-day visa-free program (which runs through end of 2026 for 45 other countries).

**2026 caveats:** Algeria NOT on the 45-country visa-free list. Ordinary-passport Algerians must apply via CVASC; only diplomatic/official Algerian passports get 30-day visa-free access [17].

**Sources:** [13] travelchinaguide.com/embassy/algeria.htm · [14] embbeijing.mfa.gov.dz · [15] travelchinaguide.com/embassy/visa/tourist.htm · [16] china-briefing.com/news/china-visa-free-travel-policies-complete-guide · [17] discover.passportindex.org (China visa-free extension to end-2026)

### 6. Russia · Tourist visa with voucher

**Where:** Embassy of the Russian Federation, Chemin du Prince d'Annam, El-Biar, Alger. **No outsourced VAC for Algerian applicants**, no BLS/VFS partner. Direct embassy submission [18].

**Visa types:** Tourist (single/double entry, max 30 days typical), business (with invitation from Russian MFA), private/study/work (out of scope).

**Document checklist (headline) for tourist:**
- Passport valid at least 3 weeks beyond Russian visa expiry (Algiers consulate variance; standard worldwide rule is 6 months) [19]
- Completed Russian visa application form (online portal print) [19]
- Recent passport photo (3.5x4.5cm, white bg, ICAO compliant)
- **Tourist invitation/voucher** issued by a Russian travel agency accredited by the Russian Federal Tourism Agency — the voucher is mandatory, must include traveler's full info + itinerary + entry/exit dates [19][20]
- Hotel confirmation matching the voucher
- Travel medical insurance valid in Russia for full stay duration
- Proof of funds

**Processing time range:** 5-10 working days standard at Algiers embassy; expedited service may exist [UNVERIFIED — not confirmed in retrievable official channel; recommend caveat language: "selon ambassade, 1 à 3 semaines en général"].

**Biometric:** [UNVERIFIED — Russian embassies began rolling out fingerprint collection in 2015+ in select missions. Algiers embassy policy as of 2026 not retrievable from official channels.]

**e-visa:** **No.** Algeria is NOT on Russia's current 64-country unified e-visa list. The pre-2020 limited Far Eastern Federal District program once included Algeria but is superseded. Ordinary-passport Algerians must apply for a sticker visa [21][22].

**2026 caveats:** Russia is sanctioned by many flight carriers; routing options for Algerians are increasingly limited. Voucher issuance speed (1-2 days from accredited agency) is the upstream bottleneck.

**Sources:** [18] embassyinformation.com/embassy/algeria/russia (address) · [19] russiable.com/visa-to-russia/from-algeria · [20] visa-invitation-russie.com (voucher process) · [21] evisa.kdmid.ru (official MFA e-visa portal — 64 countries listed, Algeria absent) · [22] en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Russia (e-visa eligibility list)

### 7. Egypt · Embassy sticker (with VOA exception)

**Where:** Embassy of Egypt, Alger (no outsourced VAC). Direct embassy submission for individual applicants [23].

**Visa types:** Tourist (single/multi entry), business. New multi-entry 5-year visa initiative announced for Algerians (allowing stays up to 6 months) — verify embassy current rules at time of application [24].

**Document checklist (headline):**
- Passport valid 6+ months from arrival date [25]
- Embassy application form
- 2 recent passport photos
- Confirmed return flight booking
- Hotel reservation / accommodation proof
- Bank statements (3-6 months typical)
- Employer letter / proof of professional status
- For organized group tours: tour operator guarantee letter (enables visa-on-arrival pathway — see below) [25]
- Children under 14: visa-exempt entirely [25]

**Processing time range:** 5-10 working days standard at Algiers embassy [UNVERIFIED specific 2026 timeline — official embassy site content not retrievable; recommend caveat copy].

**Biometric:** No standard biometric step for the embassy sticker as of latest verifiable information.

**e-visa:** **No.** Algeria is NOT on Egypt's e-visa eligibility list per current Egyptian visa policy [25].

**2026 caveats:** **Visa-on-arrival is allowed when traveling as part of an organized tour group of 2+ people with a signed travel-agency guarantee letter** — this is a specific exception that an Algerian agency (like Alliance) can leverage by issuing the guarantee letter for its group clients [25]. The bilateral 5-year multi-entry initiative announced 2024/2025 may be activated for individual applicants — confirm with embassy. Adults outside an organized group must pre-arrange visa via embassy [24][25].

**Sources:** [23] visahq.com/egypt/embassy/algeria · [24] travelandtourworld.com (Egypt-Algeria multi-entry initiative) · [25] en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visa_policy_of_Egypt (official rules summary)

### 8. Saudi Arabia · Tourist + Umrah note

**Where (tourism):** Saudi Arabia's general tourist e-visa is **not open to Algerian ordinary passports directly**. Algerians who hold a valid Schengen / UK / US tourist or business visa (with entry stamp), or GCC residency, become e-visa eligible via visa.visitsaudi.com [26][27]. Algerians without any of those must apply at the Saudi Embassy / Consulate (Algiers).

**Where (Umrah):** Channelled through ONPO-licensed (Office National du Pèlerinage et de la Omra) and Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah-approved Algerian operators. Direct individual Umrah visa not available to Algerians — must go through a licensed agency tied to the Nusuk Umrah platform (umrah.nusuk.sa) [28][29].

**Visa types in scope:** Tourist e-visa (conditional eligibility above), Umrah visa (via licensed operator), business visit (via inviter in KSA), family visit. Hajj is **EXCLUDED FROM AGENCY SCOPE** — Hajj is monopoly-organized by ONPO + ministry quota [29].

**Document checklist (headline) — tourist e-visa (conditional eligibility):**
- Passport 6+ months validity
- Recent personal photo (digital)
- Valid Schengen/UK/US visa + entry stamp OR GCC residency
- Confirmed accommodation (hotel booking via Nusuk-linked partner now mandatory)
- Travel insurance bundled into the e-visa fee
- Email + payment card for the e-visa portal

**Document checklist (headline) — Umrah via licensed operator:**
- Passport 6+ months validity, 2 blank pages
- Recent photos (white bg)
- Vaccination certificates per current Saudi MoH rules (meningitis ACWY mandatory; seasonal flu/COVID per current Saudi MoH guidance)
- Marriage certificate if traveling with spouse; Mahram documentation rules apply for women under 45 per current Saudi rules [UNVERIFIED current 2026 mahram threshold — Saudi has relaxed this; confirm with operator]
- Round-trip flight + hotel + ground transport, all pre-booked through Nusuk-integrated providers
- Nusuk app registration mandatory

**Processing time range:** Tourist e-visa (conditional eligibility): 24h-72h typical post-submission. Umrah via operator: highly variable, depends on operator queue + Saudi quota — typically 2-4 weeks.

**Biometric:** Tourist e-visa: no in-person biometric. Umrah/embassy sticker: increasingly biometric — varies by route [UNVERIFIED specific Algiers embassy 2026 procedure].

**e-visa:** Tourist e-visa: **conditional only** (Schengen/UK/US visa or GCC residency required) [26][27].

**2026 caveats:** As of 01 Feb 2025, Saudi Arabia **suspended one-year multiple-entry tourist visas for 14 nationalities including Algeria**, replacing with single-entry valid max 30 days. Tourist/business/family visit visas for these 14 nationalities are also **temporarily suspended during Hajj season** (typically April through mid-June) — relevant year-round planning [27][29]. Umrah visa entry deadlines for 2026 season set: final entry 03 April 2026, final departure 18 April 2026 (Ramadan + immediate aftermath block) [29]. **Hajj is OUT OF SCOPE for Alliance Travel** — only ONPO and ministry-licensed Hajj operators may legally organize Hajj from Algeria; Umrah may be facilitated subject to ONPO/Ministry licensing rules (flag this caveat).

**Sources:** [26] saudi-arabia-evisas.com (eligibility list referencing Schengen/UK/US visa pathway) · [27] jobbatical.com (Saudi visa nationality rules 2026, single-entry conversion) · [28] umrah.nusuk.sa · [29] news.radioalgerie.dz (ONPO Umrah deadlines 2026)

### 9. United States · B1/B2 visitor visa

**Where:** **US Embassy Algiers** — in-person interview at the consular section. DS-160 submitted online via ceac.state.gov, MRV fee paid via ustraveldocs.com, appointment booked via the same portal [30].

**Visa types in scope:** B1 (business), B2 (tourism/medical), B1/B2 combined. F/M/J (student/exchange), H/L (work) all out of scope of "rendez-vous visa" page but mention if requested.

**Document checklist (headline):**
- Passport valid 6+ months beyond intended stay
- DS-160 confirmation page (with barcode)
- MRV visa fee payment receipt (USD 185 for B1/B2 as of last published State Department schedule)
- One digital photo (per DS-160 spec), one printed photo for in-person interview
- Appointment confirmation page (ustraveldocs.com)
- Supporting docs at the interview: employment letter, payslips, bank statements (6+ months), property/business documents, travel itinerary, hotel bookings, ties-to-Algeria proof (family certificates, property, ongoing employment)
- For minors: birth certificate + parental consent

**Processing time range:** Appointment wait at Algiers Embassy varies significantly — historically several months. After interview, processing typically 5-10 working days if approved without administrative processing; with administrative processing (Section 221(g)), can extend 4-12+ weeks. Refer applicants to ustraveldocs.com for current wait times [30].

**Biometric:** Yes — fingerprints captured at the Embassy at the time of interview (no separate VAC step).

**e-visa:** No — interview required (ESTA does NOT apply to Algerian passports; Algeria not on Visa Waiver Program).

**2026 caveats (IMPORTANT):** **As of January 21, 2026**, Algerian nationals approved for B1/B2 visas are subject to the US State Department's **B1/B2 Visa Bond Pilot Program** — applicants found otherwise eligible must post a refundable bond of USD 5,000, 10,000, or 15,000 (amount determined by the consular officer at interview). Bond paid via Pay.gov after interview; visa is not issued until bond posts. Entry must be via designated US ports of entry (commercial air + CBP preclearance only — no land/sea/charter/general-aviation). Pilot runs until 05 Aug 2026 unless extended. FIFA 2026 World Cup competitors and verified ticket holders may have the bond waived [31][32][33]. Algeria first added 06 Jan 2026, effective 21 Jan 2026.

**Sources:** [30] dz.usembassy.gov/visas (general) · travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/News/visas-news/countries-subject-to-visa-bonds.html · [31] State Dept bond program page (Algeria listed, effective 21 Jan 2026) · [32] Fragomen / Ogletree legal alerts confirming Algeria addition · [33] BAL Immigration News (38 countries roster confirmation)

### 10. Canada · TRV (Temporary Resident Visa) with biometric

**Where:** Online application via the IRCC secure account (ircc.canada.ca). Biometric collection at the **VFS Global Canada VAC, Cité Houidef Bouteldja, 3rd floor, N°1 Ben Aknoun, Algiers** (Sunday-Thursday 08:30-16:30) [34][35]. The Embassy of Canada in Algiers does **not** provide visa or immigration services — consular processing is done by an IRCC visa office abroad [35].

**Visa types:** TRV (visitor visa), Super Visa (for parents/grandparents of CDN citizens/PRs), study permit, work permit (out of scope for tourism page).

**Document checklist (headline) for TRV:**
- Passport valid throughout intended stay [36]
- IMM 5257 (Application for Visitor Visa) + IMM 5645 (Family Information) if applying with family [36]
- Digital photo to IRCC spec
- Proof of funds: 6 months bank statements, typically CAD 2,500-5,000 for short visits [36]
- Detailed travel itinerary + hotel bookings + (if visiting family) invitation letter with host status (CDN citizen / PR) and host financial documents
- Strong ties-to-Algeria proof (employment letter + property + family)
- Purpose-of-trip letter
- For minors: birth certificate + parental consent + custody documents
- Biometric instruction letter (issued after online application submission)

**Processing time range:** IRCC publishes a "TRV applied from outside Canada" estimate that varies monthly; recent ranges sit around 4-8 weeks but can extend significantly. After online submission, biometrics must be given at the VAC within 30 days of the BIL. Direct applicants to canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html for live numbers [34][37].

**Biometric:** Yes — collected at the **Algiers VAC in Ben Aknoun**. **NOT routed to Tunis or Dakar for Algerians as of 2026** per VFS Global Canada and Government of Canada Algiers info pages. Biometric fingerprint + digital photo, CAD 85 fee. Prints valid 10 years for TRV [34][35].

**e-visa:** No — TRV sticker required for Algerians (Algeria not eTA-eligible).

**Fees (2026):** TRV application CAD 100, biometric CAD 85. New VFS service charges effective 01 May 2026 [34][38].

**2026 caveats:** Consular decision processing is performed by an IRCC visa office abroad (historically the Rabat visa office network has handled North African files, but the active 2026 routing for Algerian TRV decisions is [UNVERIFIED — Canada.ca international visa offices page returned 403 during research; applicants should check the IRCC web form for the current visa-office assignment]). The Algiers Embassy explicitly states it does not provide visa services, only biometrics through the VAC and consular services for Canadian citizens [35].

**Sources:** [34] vfsglobal.ca/canada/algeria (VAC address + hours) · [35] international.gc.ca/country-pays/algeria-algerie/algiers-alger-info (Algiers embassy services scope) · [36] canada.ca IMM 5484 / IMM 5257 / IMM 5645 forms · [37] canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/check-processing-times.html · [38] sugimotovisa.com (Canada biometric fees 2026, VFS service charges note)
